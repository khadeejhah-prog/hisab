About Hisab

Hisab (حساب) means "the count". It shows what things really cost you, in your own time, before you pay. Photos are read by AI and are not kept. No bank, card, loan or investment product is ever named or promoted. Levels and certificates are recognition only. Not financial or legal advice.

Terms & Privacy

Terms of use

1. What Hisab is. Hisab is a financial-education tool. It explains what things cost in your own time and helps you pause before spending. It is not a bank, a broker, a lender, a lawyer or a financial adviser.

2. Not advice. Everything Hisab shows, including prices, "cheaper elsewhere" estimates, contract summaries, link checks and loan comparisons, is produced by an AI model and may be wrong or out of date. Check before you buy, sign or send money. Decisions are yours.

3. No products. Hisab never names, recommends or sells any bank, card, loan, buy-now-pay-later, investment, crypto or betting product, and earns nothing from what you buy.

4. Age. Hisab is for ages 7 and up. Under 13, a parent or guardian should set it up with you and may add you under Family.

5. Your conduct. Only scan what you are allowed to photograph. Never scan another person's card, ID, screen or documents without their permission. Do not use Hisab to harass, defraud or spy on anyone.

6. Availability. Hisab is provided free, as is, with no promise it will always be available or error-free. The team may change or stop it at any time.

7. Liability. To the extent the law allows, the Hisab team is not liable for any loss arising from use of Hisab, including purchases, contracts or payments made after reading its output.

Privacy

Stored only on your phone: nickname, date of birth, photo, buddy, language, pocket money or pay, goals, scans history, reminders, check-in answers, settings and passcode (as a one-way hash). None of this is uploaded. Deleting the app's data in Settings removes it all.

Sent when you scan: the photo you took, plus kid/adult, age band, language, detail level and who you are (for example "student"). It goes to our server and on to the AI provider to be read, then the answer comes back. We do not store the photo. Your money numbers are never sent.

Sent when you check a link: the link you pasted.

The anonymous log: one line per decision, for example "kid · scan · skipped · Dh449 · 15 Nov". It carries a random code your phone made up, never your name, photo or amounts you earn. Both check-ins (confidence 1 to 5 and the three short questions) are logged the same way. This is how we measure whether Hisab helps and may be shown, in aggregate, in our reports.

Who can see what: a parent who adds a child sees the child's level and badges, never their scans. Family Shield shares only the warning text you choose to send.

No accounts, no tracking: no email, no password on a server, no advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling of data.

Your rights: you can download everything (Settings → Download my record) or delete everything (Settings → Delete everything) at any time. To ask about the anonymous log, contact the Hisab team.
